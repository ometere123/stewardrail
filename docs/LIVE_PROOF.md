# Live proof index

The final-source Studionet deployment and the observed lifecycle rows are indexed in
[`LIVE_EVIDENCE.md`](LIVE_EVIDENCE.md). The five deployment transactions are
FINALIZED with successful execution, and the packet includes threshold
activation, issuer-attested evidence, and deterministic refusal. The lifecycle
packet named `live-final-hardening.json` is retained as historical evidence for
the preceding source deployment and is not used as proof for the final addresses.

The economic appeal reversals and Vault payout remain open proof obligations for
the final-source deployment. A historical appeal attempt ended in execution
error/validator disagreement and is not presented as a reversal.
No payout is claimed without a finalized terminal Court message and successful
Vault execution.
